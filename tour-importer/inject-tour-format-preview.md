### 🏷️ Tour Format
A small-group (up to 12 per booking) bus day trip from Las Vegas running 10–11 hours, with a 30-minute photo stop at Hoover Dam and 3.5 hours at Grand Canyon West covering Eagle Point and Guano Point, with the glass-bridge Skywalk, a hot lunch, a West Rim airplane flight, and a helicopter-plus-pontoon-boat ride down to the Colorado River available as paid upgrades.
