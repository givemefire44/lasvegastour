### 👤 Best For

- **Las Vegas visitors without a rental car** — hotel pickup is included from the Strip, so no driving or parking at the canyon is needed
- **Families with young children** — the tour accepts infants and is rated suitable for all fitness levels; car seats are required for children 6 and under, so bring one or contact the operator in advance
- **Travelers who want to control their spend** — the base ticket is $94 and the Skywalk, lunch, helicopter, and airplane rides are all optional add-ons, so you pick only what you want
- **Day-trippers on a tight schedule** — the 10–11 hour door-to-door format fits a single day, with 3.5 hours at the canyon rim and a photo stop at Hoover Dam on the return
- **Anyone who wants time at both rim viewpoints** — the itinerary includes Eagle Point, where the glass Skywalk extends 70 feet over the edge, and Guano Point, which looks down to the Colorado River, with 30 minutes to an hour at each
