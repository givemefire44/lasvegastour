### 🗺️ The Itinerary
A helicopter picks you up from your Las Vegas hotel and departs from the VIP Heliport.
You fly over the Hoover Dam, the Memorial Bypass Bridge, Fortification Hill, and Lake Mead.
The helicopter flies along the Grand Canyon West Rim and descends 1,500 feet below the rim over the Colorado River.
You arrive at the Grand Canyon Western Ranch and have a meal at the Ranch House.
You can add a horseback ride, a horse-drawn wagon tour, a buffalo safari, or the shooting range for an extra fee.
Dinner is served, and you stay overnight in a ranch cabin.
On day two you have breakfast and explore the ranch.
You board a Mercedes Sprinter and drive through the Joshua Tree Forest and over the Hoover Dam Bypass Bridge.
The ground transfer takes about three hours and ends with drop-off at your Las Vegas hotel.
