The whole thing is shaped to end where his music does: on the man himself.

### 🎭 The Experience
The Mentalist starring Gerry McCambridge is a one-hour mind-reading and comedy show at the V Theater inside the Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood, with tickets from **USD 39 per person**. The hour is built on Gerry working the room directly: he pulls names, secrets, and stray wishes out of audience members' heads, calls them back from the stage, and threads the reveals together with comedy between them, so the show keeps moving from one person's information to the next rather than running set magic routines. It is a single-performer act in a small Strip theater, which is the whole point. The participation is real and the people supplying the material are sitting around you, so the run of the hour depends on who is in the seats that night. **What you are paying for is sixty minutes of one man reading the room, not a staged production around him.**
